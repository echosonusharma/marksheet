# Privacy Policy

**Marksheet does not collect, store, or see any of your data. Ever.**

## The short version

When you click save, your browser sends the tab's URL, title, and timestamp directly to your own Google Sheet. That's it. Nothing goes anywhere else.

## What Marksheet can and cannot do

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Write rows to the Google Sheet you choose |
| ✅ | Read back URLs from that sheet to detect duplicates |
| ❌ | See any other files in your Google Drive |
| ❌ | Access your Google account details |
| ❌ | Send your data to any server or third party |
| ❌ | Track what pages you visit |
| ❌ | Run any analytics or telemetry |

## What stays on your device

Marksheet saves a few things in your browser's local storage (never leaves your machine):

- Your sheet settings (spreadsheet URL, tab name, column layout)
- A list of URLs already saved — so duplicate checks are instant
- A temporary Google login token (issued by Google, expires automatically)

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of the above.

## Google account access

Marksheet asks for one Google permission: the ability to read and write Google Sheets. Nothing else. No Gmail, no Drive, no calendar, no profile.

You can revoke this access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
